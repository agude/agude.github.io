I've been having a version of this conversation with friends and colleagues
pretty regularly now:

> **Me**: We aren't really hiring junior engineers anymore. And we just laid
> off a bunch of them. Kept most of the senior and staff engineers though.
>
> **Friend**: Yeah... But where are the next senior and staff engineers going
> to come from if juniors don't learn on the job?
>
> **Me**: I guess we think that's not our problem. But also, did they need
> another generation of apprentices once the [Jacquard loom][jl_wiki] was
> invented?

[jl_wiki]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine

I think most software engineers are starting to wonder about their and their
field's future with the rise of LLM coding.
